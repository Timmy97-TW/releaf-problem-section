01The threat, and who it lands on

The weather swings hardest where the farms are smallest.

Taiwan has warmed 0.29 °C per decade since 1991, nearly three times the rate before it. We built one climate volatility index from the day–night swing, the seasonal swing and how unevenly the rain falls, then laid every farm parcel on the main island over it.

No instrument on the small farm

Ms. Chen lays dried weeds over her soil before a dry spell, so the morning dew soaks in slowly. That buys about three weeks before she has to check again, a clock built from twenty-five years on one plot. Nothing on her farm measures stress; she reads it.

Flash droughts are now arriving faster: nearly half develop inside five days. A three-week clock does not have to break for the crop to be lost. It only has to run slow once.

02The gap, and what it costs

Bioprotectants already work. They are made for a warehouse, not a one-hectare farm.

Biological protectants that hold a plant through heat, drought and pathogen pressure are sold in Taiwan today. To use one, a grower buys it from somebody who owns a fermenter, a cold chain and a registration dossier, and waits for it to come down the road.

The obvious objection“Then why not just buy them?”

Shelf life is not the reason. A dried spore sachet keeps at room temperature and is cheap. The reason is timing and dose. A bottle is made months before anyone knows what the season will do, sized for an average field, and delivered on the distributor’s schedule. A drought that forms in five days does not wait for an order, and a one-hectare farm cannot hold stock for every stress it might meet.

03The catch

Making it on the farm puts live engineered bacteria on the farm.

Move the fermenter to the field and the organism comes with it. A farm has no containment building, no autoclave and no biosafety officer, and our chassis, B. subtilis 168, forms spores. The protectant has to get into the soil. The bacteria that make it must not.

  1. Cells stay behind the wall. 2 mL of harvest-side medium plated on LB agar grew 0 colonies. One plating cycle 1, 6 June 2026. Replicates and log reduction are not measured.
  2. Nobody opens the vessel. Every opening is a containment event, so the loop is harvested through the membrane and read by an in-line photometer.
  3. A leak counts as a breach. Cycle 1 leaked at the pump head. We logged it as lost containment, and the next build changed because of it.
  4. A regulator has to agree. Open No Taiwan regulator has yet confirmed a contained-use classification for a device like this.

So the machine has to be

  • Sealedclosed from inoculation to shutdown
  • Biosafethe product leaves, the organism does not

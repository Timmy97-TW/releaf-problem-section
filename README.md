# ReLeaf homepage: problem section (draft)

Draft of the problem section for the ReLeaf (GEMS Taiwan, iGEM 2026) wiki homepage, built to sit between the hero and the dark act.

| Section | What it argues | What it asks for |
|---|---|---|
| 01 The threat | Climate volatility falls on the smallest farms | On demand, right time, right amount |
| 02 The gap | Production is contained and far, or close and uncontained; nothing is both | On site, contained |
| 03 The distance | A drought forms in five days; a new biological takes about five years to reach a farmer | A shorter wait, control in farmers' hands |
| Handoff | The nine-step chain collapses onto the farmer and becomes ReLeaf | all of the above |

To bake in: drop the STANDALONE block in `assets/css/problem.css`, keep the `.pb-` and `.jr-` rules, and don't ship the copied `tokens.css`. Set `--pb-offset: var(--nav-h)` so the one-screen sections account for the fixed nav. Map layers match `wiki/assets/img/bigpicture/map/`.

Run locally with `python3 -m http.server`.
